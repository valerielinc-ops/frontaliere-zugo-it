Apply today for the position of Working Student - Cybersecurity Governance (OT/IT) 40–60% and start as soon as possible – your team is looking forward to meeting you! Your new responsibilities

  • Support risk assessments of production-critical systems based on internal standards aligned with ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF 2.0 and IEC 62443
  • Support security assessments of critical suppliers, review security requirements and contracts, and help establish incident escalation paths and SLAs
  • Contribute to disaster recovery and business continuity planning
  • Foster collaboration between IT, OT, production and global cybersecurity teams
  • Develop and improve vulnerability management processes
  • Ensure the accuracy and completeness of our application and asset inventory
  • Create audit-ready factory IT manuals, processes and documentation Your talents and experience
  • Enrolled in at least the 4th semester of a bachelor's programme in the area of policy and compliance, science, or technology at a Swiss university
  • Basic knowledge of information security concepts (risk management, CIA triad)
  • Ability to communicate technical requirements to non-technical stakeholders
  • Structured, analytical working style
  • Full professional fluency in German, with the ability to communicate comfortably in English
  • Nice to have: knowledge of ISO/IEC 27001, NIST CSF, IEC 62443 or OT environments Employee benefits
  • Mobile working from anywhere within Switzerland
  • Opportunity to develop an international professional network
  • Gain experience at one of the largest tech employers in Switzerland

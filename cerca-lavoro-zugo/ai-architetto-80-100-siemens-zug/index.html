Descrizione completa

Together with our customers, we combine the real and digital worlds With technology, software and services for smart buildings, we transform the everyday for everyone. By combining the real and digital worlds, we empower our customers to reach their decarbonization goals, lower their operating costs and create world-class occupant experiences for their people. It’s not just about improving buildings – it’s about creating efficient, safe, adaptable and sustainable environments, which improve the way the world lives and works. At Siemens Smart Infrastructure, Buildings Products (SI B PRO), we are shaping the future of building automation through Artificial Intelligence, cloud technologies, and modern software platforms. Our teams build assistants and automated workflows that help engineers design, configure, and commission building automation systems. As an AI Architect, you will define the technical architecture for customer-facing AI products, and guide our product teams in their implementation If you enjoy turning AI prototypes into secure, maintainable products that run on AWS or portable cloud-native platforms, we would like to meet you. You will own the architecture, standards, and technical decisions for our customer-facing AI products on AWS and portable cloud-native platforms. Working with product managers, AI platform engineers, software architects, and cybersecurity teams, you will establish reusable reference patterns and guides so product teams build AI features in a consistent way. This is a hands-on architecture role. You will build Python prototypes and reference code to test key architectural decisions before teams build them at scale, and support AI platform engineers and product developers through design reviews and hard technical problems. Would you also like to play an active role in influencing current trends and developments and make a positive impact on the lives of millions of people in Switzerland? Then you’ve come to the right place! Apply today for the position of AI Architect your team is looking forward to meeting you! Your new responsibilities

  • Define and maintain the technical roadmap and architecture standards for AI products across SI B PRO
  • Design architectures for LLM applications, RAG, and multi-agent workflows
  • Define how AI systems connect to engineering tools, product APIs, and MCP servers
  • Define how teams measure AI quality, run offline and online evaluations, and trace errors in production
  • Design for multi-tenancy, strict data isolation, portability, and low latency
  • Define cost targets and FinOps practices for AI products
  • Lead threat modeling to stop prompt injection, data leaks, and unsafe tool access
  • Work with security and legal teams to meet privacy, data residency, and rules such as the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, and ISO 27001
  • Write reusable reference patterns and guides so product teams build AI features in a consistent way
  • Evaluate AI models, agent frameworks, and vendors, and recommend whether to build or buy each component
  • Build Python prototypes and reference code to test key architectural decisions before teams build them at scale
  • Support AI platform engineers and product developers through design reviews and hard technical problems Your talents and experience
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field
  • At least 7 years of experience in software architecture, cloud architecture, or AI technical leadership, including production AI or cloud products
  • Deep knowledge of LLM architectures, RAG, agent workflows, tool use, memory, and human review loops
  • Knowledge of MCP deployment and security patterns for production systems
  • Proven experience with secure, multi-tenant cloud systems, including IAM, networking, monitoring, and IaC (experience on AWS or portable stacks based on CNCF projects is preferred; equivalent experience on other clouds is welcome)
  • Working knowledge of AI threat modeling, privacy, data residency, and regulations such as the EU AI Act, ISO 42001, and ISO 27001
  • Strong Python skills and experience with REST APIs and event-driven integrations so you can write reference code
  • Experience in any of these areas is a plus:
  • AWS (including Amazon Bedrock) or portable cloud-native stacks built on CNCF projects
  • Equivalent AI and cloud concepts from other cloud providers such as Azure or Google Cloud
  • Building automation or industrial engineering domains
  • Knowledge graphs and enterprise data modeling
  • AI lifecycle and evaluation platforms
  • Docker and Kubernetes
  • Interest in new programming languages like Rust
  • Ability to make practical trade-offs between long-term architecture and short-term product delivery
  • Ability to explain complex technical choices clearly to engineers, product managers, and executives
  • Ownership of decisions from early design through production launch

Responsabilità principali

  • Main Responsibilities
  • The Operational Technology Engineer plays a critical role in standardizing and optimizing automation systems to capture, analyse, and leverage production data, driving operational efficiency across all industrial processes. Strategy & Standardization
  • Collaborate in define and implement corporate-wide ICS/TIS standards to ensure global consistency in KPIs, reporting structures, and data architecture across 100+ plants
  • Align OT strategy in line with corporate guidance with business goals, identifying emerging technology needs and establishing multi-year technical roadmaps.
  • Drive innovation and industrial transformation: implement Plant of Tomorrow (PoT) solutions to improve efficiency, productivity, and competitiveness.
  • Train and mentor the OT plant teams: build high-performing operational plant teams through training and development, focusing on ICS and TIS systems.
  • Educate and advise plant OT related risks and opportunities
  • Support the product roadmap for new functionalities and features within the PCS/MES ecosystem.
  • Design and maintain robust interfaces between the shop floor (PCS/DCS), laboratory (LIMS), and business layers (SAP). Global Deployment & Operations
  • Support the full lifecycle of ICS/MES deployments, from initial site assessment and vendor coordination to post-go-live support and version upgrades.

Requisiti principali

  • Level of education/qualifications normally required:
  • MS or PhD in Electrical, Electronic, or Automation Engineering.
  • (preferred) ISA/IEC 62443 OT cybersecurity certificate Experience
  • 3 years’ experience as plant automation/systems engineer for process industry
  • 3 years’ in managing industrial data systems (Historians, MES) and industrial networking.
  • 3 years’ experience in cement (advantage) Technical / functional skills:
  • Deep understanding of OT systems in the process industry: This includes industrial control systems, SCADA systems, distributed control systems (DCS), programmable logic controllers (PLCs), and manufacturing execution systems.
  • Understanding of OT security threats, vulnerabilities, and mitigation strategies.
  • Familiarity with security frameworks and standards like IEC 62443.
  • Knowledge of industrial networking protocols like Ethernet/IP, Profinet, Modbus, and OPC UA.
  • Knowledge of cloud platforms and IoT technologies relevant to industrial environments, JavaScript and prototyping languages such as Python
  • In-depth understanding of continuous cement manufacturing processes, instrumentation, and sensor technologies. Language skills:
